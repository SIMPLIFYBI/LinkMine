## Info

- Data area (2026-11-22): `/admin/intelligence/data`. The published series layer has 554 selectable series (524 mining, 8 petroleum, 22 power) from 20,719 series-country rows; only series with 3 or more countries are kept, because 17 of the 21 mining statistics exist for one country only. Series are never merged across source, basis or unit; the user picks the basis. Share of total is offered only where `share_ok` (not for cement, mica, rare earths, aggregates, or group totals). No world total is published, so shares are of the reporting countries. Per person uses World Bank population, which covers 2020 to 2025 only, and the period clamps to it. Map colours use a fixed scale per selection, anchored at the 5th percentile and labelled.
- Series API cache is keyed on the data refresh time (`v`), after a stale five-minute copy hid the Congo fix during testing. Anything that caches published reads must do the same.
- Not built from the brief yet: region presets, per-GDP normalisation, "breakdown" by commodity or year, asset (pin) maps. Asset maps belong in the Atlas because mined assets exist for only two countries; chart and map-of-statistics share a geography, asset maps do not.
- EIA coverage break (2026-11-22): in total petroleum and other liquids production, 1993 to 2003 leave non-producing countries blank (about 85 to 88 countries a year have a value, no zero rows), while 1990 to 1992 and 2004 onward record explicit zeros. The published country count therefore jumps from about 87 (2003) to about 172 (2004). It is a recording convention, not a change in the world. Documented in the comment on `published.energy_self_sufficiency` and shown as a note on the home page map.
- `published.energy_self_sufficiency` (liquids and gas, 9,026 rows, 206 countries) is built on `serving.v_energy_self_sufficiency`, so the no-trade placeholder rule is inherited. It reproduces the Energy Flip crossings exactly (China 1993, UK 2006, US 2022).
- Map geometry is Natural Earth 1:110m (174 countries plus 16 hand-placed micro-state dots), generated by `scripts/build-world-paths.mjs` into `lib/intelligence/world-paths.json`. The database holds no boundary geometry (`core.jurisdiction.boundary` is empty for every row checked). Historical states (Soviet Union, Yugoslavia, Czechoslovakia, East Germany) cannot be drawn, so the hero map starts in 1992. No disputed-border disclaimer has been added yet.
- Caption wording about Brazil's pre-salt fields and US shale comes from the Energy Flip report, not from the data.
- `REPORT_BUILDER_BRIEF.md` figures were re-checked against the live database and hold. Key constraint for the Data area: chart mode (about 200 countries) and map mode (mining assets in only 2 countries; lithium has 1 mappable asset) have different geographies and must not share a country picker.
- Embed (`/embed/reserves`, 2026-11-22) is public and cached (`s-maxage` 1 h plus a 5 min in-memory copy). It is hand-written vanilla JS, so it duplicates the chart logic in the React explorer; keep the two in step when the chart changes. It loads IBM Plex from Google Fonts (a third-party request from the embedding page). Embed code generated on localhost contains localhost links, so generate snippets from the production site.
- Verified after exposing `published` (2026-11-22): anon reads all 323 `reserves_to_production` rows; anon `refresh_all` and an insert into `rp_basis` are both refused (401); the `prov` schema stays closed to anon (406). Do not run Supabase's suggested `GRANT ALL ... TO anon` step for this schema; grants are deliberately select-only.
- `mining.resource_estimate` (5,190 rows) and `mining.production_period` (22,336 rows) are no longer empty as of 2026-11-22 (an earlier version of this log said 0 rows). Contents not yet reviewed, so site-level resources and production are still not shown.
- No market-capitalisation data exists anywhere in the database.
- Exploration expenditure covers Australian jurisdictions only (mining 8, petroleum 7), in AUD.
- Pink Sheet prices (source S5, CC BY) are registered but not loaded.
- The handoff document was stale on counts (23 serving views, 59 commercial series); the live database has 25 views and matviews and 58 commercial series.
- Verified: the reserves-to-production logic reproduces 28 of 29 report commodities exactly (country counts, Australian ranks and years). Minimum rule is 5 countries with both measures.
